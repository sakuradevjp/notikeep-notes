FAQ

Can NotiKeep see notifications I received before I installed it?

No. NotiKeep only saves notifications that arrive after it's installed and Notification Access is granted.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 9.0 and newer.

How much storage does NotiKeep use?

Text notifications are tiny — a few MB covers months. Storage is mostly images: each saved photo is around 20–80 KB. A heavy user with 1,000 image-bearing notifications a month uses ~30–80 MB. If you're concerned, shorten the retention period.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

All NotiKeep data — notifications, images, preferences, bookmarks — is stored on-device, so uninstalling deletes it. To keep it across a reinstall or new phone, consider Backup & Restore (Pro feature) first.

What permissions does NotiKeep need?

Just notification access. No location, no contacts, no other access.

Can I export my data?

Pro creates a ZIP with the database, saved images, bookmarks, mute lists, and settings — all in one. Restore on a new phone in one step. The save location is up to you.

What happens at the end of the 30-day trial?

You revert to Free. No auto-renewal. If you want Pro, pick a plan from the upgrade screen.

Will it drain my battery or slow my phone?

NotiKeep only does work when a notification arrives. It doesn't sit there draining your battery.

Where is my data stored?

All data stays on-device. Backups go only to a path you choose.
